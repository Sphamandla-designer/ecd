Typography

Quicksand names things, Inter explains them. Two families, no exceptions.

H1 — Page title · Quicksand SemiBold 24/32
H2 — Section title · 20/28
H3 — Card & dialog title · 18/24
H4 — Form label, list title · 16/22
Body — Inter Regular 16/24. The default for reading text, dialog copy and long descriptions.
Help — Inter Regular 14/20. Help text, list subtitles, app-bar subtitles, error messages.
Caption — Inter Regular 12/16.
