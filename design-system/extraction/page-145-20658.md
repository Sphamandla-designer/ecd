## 1. Page overview

Page 23 is the design spec for the **DBE Registration Helper** — the in-app flow that walks an ECD
(Early Childhood Development) preschool principal through registering their programme with the South
African **Department of Basic Education (DBE)** so the centre can qualify for the ECD subsidy.

The page is organised into **7 numbered, "Approved"-stamped sections**. Each is introduced by a wide
black `Header` banner instance spanning the canvas (3568 × 300 px; heading text at x=120, y=77,
146 px tall; a green `#4ADE4A`-family "Approved" status pill sits 60 px to the right of the heading).

| # | Header node | Section title | Canvas band (y) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `145:21495` | **1. Initial form** | 0 – 5,635 |
| 2 | `145:21496` | **2. Landing screen variations** | 5,635 – 7,034 |
| 3 | `145:21497` | **3. Info screens** | 7,034 – 9,050 |
| 4 | `145:21505` | **4. Update my stage** | 9,050 – 10,665 |
| 5 | `145:21579` | **5. Notifications** | 10,665 – 12,138 |
| 6 | `145:21596` | **6. Add to Community > Resources** | 12,138 – 13,536 |
| 7 | `145:21597` | **7. Trial user exception** | 13,536 – end |

All phone artboards are **360 px wide**; height is 640 px for fixed screens and 706 / 750 / 942 /
952 / 1035 / 1066 / 1076 px where the screen scrolls.

### Domain model

The helper models DBE registration as a **two-stage ladder**:

| Stage | Outcome | Info screen |
|---|---|---|
| **Apply** | **Bronze certificate** | `145:21231` |
| **Comply** | **Silver certificate** (meet the standards) | `145:21350` |

A third certificate tier, **Gold**, exists in the certificate picker (`145:21394`, `145:21408`) but has
no dedicated info screen on this page.

Both stages appear on the **Business → Registration** tab as a vertical stepper with a per-stage
trailing pill button (`Info` when not started, `View` when achieved).
