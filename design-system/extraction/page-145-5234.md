## Page overview

"Page 22" contains the complete **classroom programme-planning feature** of the ECD Connect mobile app (screen codes `WO6.x`, plus the entry-point `WO2.2.0` Children/Classroom dashboard). It covers:

1. **Programme dashboard** — day-by-day "Activities" view per class, with routine slots (Greeting time & message board, Small group activity, Free play, Large group activity, Story & activity).
2. **Setting up a themed programme** (WO6.1.x) — choose a theme, choose dates & language ("Timing"), confirmation dialog, view/edit auto-planned activities.
3. **"No theme" manual planning** (WO6.2.x) — choose/view small group, large group, story and story-activity screens, in selected/none-selected states.
4. **Planning nudges & guides** (WO6.3.x) — start-planning nudges, plan-next-week, streaks, missing skills, "What are children working on?" pop-up, weekend/holiday messages.
5. **Onboarding & help** (WO6.5.x) — question-mark info dialogs, a 10-step walkthrough, "Programme best practices" info screens (daily routine, learning through play, developing children holistically).
6. **Permission variants** — practitioner vs principal views, and read-only variants for practitioners without add/edit permission (W3).
