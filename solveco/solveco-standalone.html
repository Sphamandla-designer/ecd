About SolveCo

An independent actuarial consultancy serving insurers since 2006

What we do

SolveCo provides actuarial and business solutions to the insurance and financial industries, with a focus on the life assurance, pensions, health and short-term insurance sectors. The work centres on practical support: implementing and verifying administration systems, developing and pricing products, modelling and valuing liabilities, and preparing data for migration.

Led by founder and Managing Director Mia Geringer, working alongside clients' own actuarial teams.

How we work

Specialist support that fits the way your business already runs

We understand the business first, communicate transparently and leave clients with documented work they can own.

Working alongside client teams

  1. Understand the business before recommending solutions

    Time is spent understanding how a client's business operates before any solution is recommended or implemented.

  2. Communicate transparently

    Clear, open communication throughout an engagement, so clients know what is being done, why, and where a project stands.

  3. Allocate resources to the project at hand

    Support is shaped around the specific project, including interim resourcing and project-specific capacity.

  4. Document the work and transfer the knowledge

    Best-practice reporting and documentation, so the outcome and the understanding behind it stay with the client.

  5. Work alongside client actuarial teams

    Integration with in-house actuarial teams to complete projects on time, complementing existing capability rather than replacing it.
