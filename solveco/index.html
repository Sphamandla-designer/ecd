How we work

Specialist support that fits the way your business already runs

SolveCo describes its approach in terms of understanding the business first, communicating transparently and leaving clients with documented work they can own. The principles below paraphrase that published description.

Paraphrased from solveco.co.za (opens in a new tab). Not a formal framework, methodology or guarantee.

  1. Understand the business before recommending solutions

    Time is spent understanding how a client's business operates before any solution is recommended or implemented, so that the work addresses the actual problem.

  2. Communicate transparently

    Clear, open communication throughout an engagement, so clients know what is being done, why, and where a project stands.

  3. Allocate resources to the project at hand

    Support is shaped around the specific project, including interim resourcing and project-specific capacity rather than a one-size-fits-all team.

  4. Document the work and transfer the knowledge

    Best-practice reporting and documentation, so the outcome, and the understanding behind it, stay with the client after the engagement ends.

  5. Work alongside client actuarial teams

    Integration with in-house actuarial teams to complete projects on time, complementing existing capability rather than replacing it.
