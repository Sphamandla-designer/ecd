The Flex Academy method

From first unit to operating company.

The same progression the founders worked through building The Flex — and then systemised in Base360. Launch covers the foundations; Scale builds the company on top.

  1. 01

    Find & evaluate opportunities

    Judge a unit, a market and a deal before you commit.

  2. 02

    Structure the business

    Choose an operating model that can hold more than one unit.

  3. 03

    Pricing & revenue processes

    Set rates, distribute across channels, track what you earn.

  4. 04

    Repeatable operations

    Turn check-ins, cleans and maintenance into routines.

  5. 05

    Systems, team & automation

    Hand work to software and people, not to your evenings.

  6. 06

    Foundations for growth

    Build the structure to add units without adding chaos.
